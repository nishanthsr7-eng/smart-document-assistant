CONTRACTOR PERSONNEL HANDBOOK
Nonfederal Support Services Agreement -- Attachment C

SECTION 1. SCOPE

This handbook governs contractor personnel assigned under the Nonfederal Support
Services Agreement. Contractor personnel are not Federal employees and are not
covered by the Federal leave statutes. Where a provision of this handbook appears
to parallel a Federal entitlement, the contractor provision governs contractor
personnel and the Federal provision governs Federal employees. Nothing in this
handbook confers a Federal benefit.

SECTION 2. PAID TIME OFF ACCRUAL

Contractor personnel accrue paid time off on the following schedule, credited at
the close of each biweekly pay period in which the individual is in a pay status:

  Less than 2 years of continuous service .............. 3 hours per pay period
  2 years but less than 10 years of service ............ 5 hours per pay period
  10 years or more of continuous service ............... 7 hours per pay period

Part-time contractor personnel accrue paid time off on a prorated basis of 1 hour
for each 25 hours in a pay status. Temporary personnel engaged for fewer than 90
consecutive days accrue 2 hours per pay period and forfeit any unused balance at
the close of the engagement.

SECTION 3. SICK AND MEDICAL ABSENCE

Contractor personnel accrue 3 hours of sick absence credit for each biweekly pay
period in a pay status, without regard to length of service. Sick absence credit
is not transferable, is not payable on separation, and does not convert to
retirement credit.

A contractor employee may use up to 56 hours of accrued sick absence credit in a
benefit year to care for a family member with a serious health condition. A
contractor employee may use up to 24 hours of accrued sick absence credit in a
benefit year for funeral or bereavement purposes, including travel.

SECTION 4. EXTENDED FAMILY AND MEDICAL ABSENCE

Contractor personnel who have completed 18 months of continuous service and at
least 1,500 hours in a pay status are eligible for up to 10 weeks of unpaid
extended family and medical absence in a rolling 12-month period. This benefit is
contractual and is not an entitlement under the Family and Medical Leave Act.
Extended absence beyond 10 weeks requires written approval of the Contracting
Officer Representative and is granted at the sole discretion of the contractor.

SECTION 5. HOURS OF WORK

The standard contractor duty day is 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., Monday through Friday,
with a 30-minute unpaid meal period. Core hours during which all contractor
personnel must be present are 10 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. Alternate schedules require
advance written approval and may not reduce the standard 40-hour week.

SECTION 6. DOCUMENTATION

A request for absence exceeding 3 consecutive duty days must be supported by
written documentation from a licensed practitioner, submitted within 10 calendar
days of return to duty. Absences of 3 days or fewer may be self-certified.
Failure to furnish documentation within the stated period renders the absence
unauthorized and unpaid.
