ETHICS GUIDANCE ADDENDUM
Advisory Memorandum for Non-Career Appointees and Advisory Committee Members

PURPOSE

This addendum provides supplemental guidance for non-career appointees and members
of chartered advisory committees. It does not amend the Standards of Ethical
Conduct, and where this addendum and the Standards address the same subject, the
Standards control for employees to whom they apply.

INTERMITTENT ADVISORY SERVICE

A member of a chartered advisory committee who serves on an intermittent basis and
who is expected to serve no more than 60 days during any consecutive 365-day period
is treated as an intermittent advisor for the purposes of this addendum. An
intermittent advisor who exceeds 60 days of service in a 365-day period must be
redesignated, and the designation is reviewed by the Designated Agency Ethics
Official at the close of each quarter.

GIFTS AND COURTESIES

An intermittent advisor may accept unsolicited courtesies having an aggregate market
value of 25 dollars or less per source per occasion, and not exceeding 75 dollars
from any one source in a calendar year. These limits are specific to intermittent
advisors and do not establish a limit applicable to any other category of personnel.

An intermittent advisor may not accept a courtesy of any value from a source that is
currently a party to, or is seeking, a contract or grant on which the advisor is
providing advice. This prohibition applies regardless of the aggregate limits above.

OUTSIDE ACTIVITY

An intermittent advisor may engage in outside employment without prior approval
provided the activity does not relate to any matter on which the advisor has given
or is expected to give advice. Prior written approval is required where the outside
activity involves a party identified in the preceding paragraph.

TRAVEL AND SUBSISTENCE

An intermittent advisor attending a committee meeting away from the residence of the
advisor may be reimbursed for travel and subsistence at the rate established for
intermittent personnel. Reimbursement is contingent on submission of a voucher
within 30 calendar days of the close of travel.
