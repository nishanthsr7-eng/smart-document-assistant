STATE EMPLOYEE LEAVE SUPPLEMENT
Chapter 4 -- Leave Administration for State-Funded Positions

4.1 APPLICABILITY

This supplement applies to employees in state-funded positions. It does not apply
to Federal employees, to contractor personnel, or to employees whose positions are
funded under a Federal grant unless the grant instrument expressly adopts this
chapter. Where this supplement and a Federal policy address the same subject, the
funding source of the position determines which governs.

4.2 ANNUAL LEAVE

Employees in state-funded positions earn annual leave each biweekly pay period
according to length of creditable state service:

  Under 3 years of service ............................. 5 hours per pay period
  3 years but under 15 years of service ................ 7 hours per pay period
  15 years or more of service .......................... 9 hours per pay period

There is no minimum-hours rule for state annual leave accrual. An employee in a
pay status for any part of a pay period earns the full accrual for that period,
which differs from the Federal treatment of partial pay periods.

4.3 SICK LEAVE

Employees earn 5 hours of sick leave per biweekly pay period. Accrued sick leave
may be used for personal illness, for medical appointments, and for the care of a
family member, subject to the annual caps in section 4.4.

4.4 ANNUAL CAPS ON SICK LEAVE USE

  Care of a family member with a serious health condition ...... 240 hours
  Bereavement, funeral attendance, and related travel ........... 40 hours
  Care of a family member with a routine illness ................ 80 hours

Caps run on the state fiscal year, which begins 1 July, and unused capped amounts
do not carry forward.

4.5 FAMILY AND MEDICAL LEAVE

Eligible employees may take up to 14 weeks of job-protected family and medical
leave in a 24-month period. The state entitlement runs concurrently with any
Federal FMLA entitlement for which the employee separately qualifies; an employee
does not receive both entitlements in addition to one another.

4.6 OFFICE HOURS

State-funded offices are open to the public from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday
through Friday, excluding state holidays. Employees working a compressed schedule
must still provide coverage during the published public hours.
